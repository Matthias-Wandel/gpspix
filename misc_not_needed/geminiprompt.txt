Left click on the image shall select it, right click shall pop up a context menu for entries "view image" -- which will pop up a separate browser window with the image,  "view directory" which will pop up the index.html from the directory that the image is in (just assume the corresponding index.html exists) and "view map" which will pop up the coordinates of the image in the preview image in google maps in a separate window.

Make the dots for the image blue and 8 pixels in diameter. If another image falls within the size of the dot, instead of showing overlapping dots, merge them into one dot with twice the area. As there are about 15000 images with coordinates, it can potentially get slow.  For the sake of speed, whenever the map is panned or zoomed, make a list consisting of only images that fall no more than 5 pixels outside the current view of the window (calculate the GPS bounding coordinates based on the screen, then filter images by their gps coordinates).
